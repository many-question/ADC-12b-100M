Shooting PSS uses a 400 ps clock, 0.49 V common mode, +100 uV differential input and 10 fF per output. QN minus QP is sampled at fixed phase 70 ps; the clock's rising half-supply crossing is at 5 ps in the same returned PSS time origin. The unsaturated 65–75 ps region was selected before the noise comparison. Complex sampled PXF differential gain is `(H_VDP-H_VDN)/2`, evaluated at that exact event; magnitudes are taken after subtraction. Gains 271.5155 (TT) and 264.0650 (FS) match centered 1/2/5 uV finite differences within 0.22% at the selected phase. Bias/tolerance perturbations use their own matching finite differences.

PSF explicitly reports V/sqrt(Hz). Squaring once and integrating the positive spectrum needs no factor of two: an independent sampled 1 kohm/1 pF RC test yields 64.35950 uV versus 64.37405 uV from kT/C, a -0.0226% error. This validates units and spectral normalization, not comparator equivalence. The table uses 287216.015 Hz to Nyquist (1.25 GHz), 160 sidebands and 400 GHz maxacfreq. Increasing 80 to 160 sidebands changes results by <0.066%; 65–75 ps phase variation is <0.14%. Tighter tolerance and +50/-100 uV bias checks do not close the gap. With a common 50 fs step, 8 ns versus 16 ns settling differs by at most 1.93e-12 V (TT) and 5.31e-12 V (FS); maximum periodic endpoint residual is 2.64e-11 V. Earlier millivolt edge differences came from interpolating coarse saved time grids.

**Low-frequency equivalence remains unresolved.** Installed Spectre 21.1 help says source PSD is flattened below noisefmin, not cut off. The retained transient log auto-adjusts noisefmin to 287.216 kHz. Integrating PNoise only above this frequency is therefore provisional; extending integration to 1 Hz increases the estimate and does not repair the discrepancy. Finite-band transient sources versus folded periodic noise, finite-time decision statistics, and nonlinear evolution of noise are remaining hypotheses, not established causes. Within-level block bootstrap on the retained single seed does not explain the gap and cannot prove independence or seed robustness. Mean and finite-difference waveforms are unsaturated, but this alone does not establish small-noise linearity.

The linearized output RMS at 70 ps is about 59.14 mV (TT) and 111.58 mV (FS), larger than the roughly 27.22/26.45 mV deterministic means. RMS greater than the mean does not demonstrate small-signal failure: the mean naturally approaches zero near zero input. The local +/-1 to 5 uV gain check independently leaves linearity over a full physical noise distribution untested. Establishing that range would require state-trajectory curvature, gain over the relevant differential range and regeneration-boundary analysis. This remains an unresolved concern, not a demonstrated explanation; the earlier 65 ps result still retains the discrepancy.

Both methods identify worse FS than TT for V32 (ratios about 1.94 and 1.91). That limited directional agreement can guide hypotheses; it does not establish design-ranking accuracy. No further large method campaign is scheduled. Full decisions, decks, source hashes, parsed records and failures are privately retained; all owned remote paths were removed after hash verification.
